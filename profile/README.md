## What is Cueword?

Cueword is a desktop app for church media teams. It listens to the preacher, recognises Bible
references and quoted verses as they are spoken, and puts them on screen: projector, livestream
(NDI), browser overlays and presentation software.

- **Offline.** Speech recognition runs on the church computer. No cloud, no audio leaves the building.
- **The operator stays in charge.** Clear finds go straight to preview; unsure ones wait for approval.
- **Works with what you have.** Any microphone, mixer channel or the computer's own sound.
- **Windows and macOS.**

Cueword is in development and not yet available. Its first module is **Cueword Scripture**.
